# Kite Launch Kit

Kite is the browser-first video editor for creators who want to edit from any device and publish faster.

## One-Liner

Kite turns one idea into a polished, platform-ready video workflow on web, mobile, and desktop.

## Short Pitch

No app store. No download. Just edit. Kite is an installable web video editor for creators who want to import media, cut the timeline, add captions, polish the look, and export locally from iOS, Android, desktop browsers, or macOS.

## Brand Voice

- Clear, fast, creator-friendly.
- Focus on outcome before feature lists.
- Say "install from the browser" instead of "download an app."
- Say "export locally" when talking about trust and control.
- Avoid heavy agency-dashboard language.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the welcome page.
2. Show the onboarding image tour first.
3. Install Kite as a PWA.
4. Import a clip or image.
5. Open Kite AI and generate hooks/captions.
6. Add captions on the timeline.
7. Export locally.
